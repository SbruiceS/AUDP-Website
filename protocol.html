Concepts

Devices that cannot afford a long conversation.

AUDP names the agreement between sensors, controllers, and gateways: small messages, frequent samples, links that damage what they carry. Wire formats stay confidential. This is the public concept model, plus mathematics already published with AUDP-ECM.

Sensor node

Measures often, with little power or time to talk.

Controller

Must not act on a value that only looks plausible.

Gateway

Collects short messages. The link into it is often the fragile one.

Research desk

AUDP-DSP studies a series. It cannot repair a frame already accepted while damaged.

The path

  1. Sense — the instrument makes a measurement.
  2. Frame — a small message, not a file.
  3. Traverse — radio, cable, or a shared channel.
  4. Verify — AUDP-ECM repairs only inside budget, then checks.
  5. Decide — use the value, or refuse it.

Public mathematics

  • Reed–Solomon over GF(256): 2e + s ≤ n − k. Public example: ReedSolomon(31, 23).
  • Binary BCH for bit-oriented links.
  • CRC-16/CCITT-FALSE as a residual check. It detects; it does not repair.
  • Burst-resilient framing by interleaving across codewords.
