The problem

The farm fails when a packet is wrong.

Every irrigation choice, controller action, and research conclusion that depends on a field sensor is only as good as the bytes that survived the link.

Where the damage happens

Soil moisture, temperature, humidity, water, crop state, and machinery condition travel by radio, long cables, and shared channels. Interference flips symbols. A burst wipes a run of bytes. A weak signal leaves a value unknown. Sending the message again is often slow, costly, or impossible.

Silent corruption

A missing reading is visible. A corrupted reading that still parses is not. It looks like a drier field, a fuller tank, a hotter afternoon. AUDP exists so the path from sensor to decision refuses to treat damage as data.

What correct has to mean

  • Damage is detected, not averaged away.
  • Repair stays inside a proven budget: 2e + s ≤ n − k, with a CRC as a second check.
  • A value that cannot be verified is rejected.
  • Noise and attackers are different problems.
