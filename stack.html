What we are building

Three pieces. One job.

A measurement that can be believed.

AUDP — the protocol family

How agricultural devices exchange small, frequent messages over constrained links. Internal specifications are not published here.

AUDP-ECM — repair the frame

Detects, corrects, and recovers damaged telemetry. Proprietary core. Public site. Bindings: Python, D.

AUDP-DSP — study the measurement

Generates, analyses, transforms, and reconstructs signals for agricultural sensing research. Public site.

Use of the software requires a written licence with ASPF.
