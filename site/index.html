Agriculture Service of Product Farm

Data that arrives complete and correct.

AUDP — the ASPF Unified Device Protocol — is the protocol family we are building so sensors, controllers, and gateways on a farm can exchange small, frequent messages when the link itself cannot be trusted.

The problem

A wrong reading is worse than a missing one. Interference, bursts, and weak signals damage the message on the way.

What we are building

AUDP for the device conversation, AUDP-ECM to repair frames, AUDP-DSP to study signals once they are intact.

What we will not claim

Correction is not security. This site does not publish the private core or a promise that every damaged frame can be saved.
