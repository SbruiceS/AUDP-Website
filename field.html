On the farm

Same failure, five places it shows up.

The crop changes. The broken assumption does not: that the number which arrived is the number the instrument made.

Soil — moisture on a long cable

A flipped byte can turn a dry bed into a reading that looks freshly irrigated. ECM repairs bounded damage or rejects the frame. DSP studies a series that survived.

Climate — weather node over radio

Retransmission costs power the node does not have. What cannot be repaired is dropped, not invented.

Water — canal and tank level

A controller that trusts a corrupted level can open a gate at the wrong time. Verification happens before hardware moves.

Machinery — condition signals

A torn window can look like a fault, or hide one. ECM protects the frame. DSP asks frequency and anomaly questions afterward.

Aerial — remote sensing handoff

Analysis of a damaged series produces confident, wrong maps. DSP research assumes the observation was earned.
